House of Evulv Identity 2

Start with "House of Evulv Identity 2 - Brand Manual.pdf". The last page explains which file to use where.

01 House of Evulv
  Every logo version in seven colourways, as SVG (vector, any size) and transparent PNG (2400 px).
  Elements: the dove (peace), the lotus and water (healing), the tree of life (evolution), the open ring and a leaf.
  Colourways for dark backgrounds: gold-and-silver, gold-and-ivory, gold, white.
  Colourways for light backgrounds: bronze, espresso, black.

02 Sentinels of Peace
  Files ending in _original are the Sentinels of Peace seal artwork as created, enhanced to high resolution:
  the full logo, the seal and the crest cut out on transparent backgrounds, the two knights (left, right and as a pair),
  and the original square artwork. These are supplied as high-resolution transparent PNG.
  seal_greyscale is for one-colour print such as newspapers.
  The horizontal lockup and wordmark come as PNG and SVG, with vector lettering in several colours.

03 Icons and favicons, 04 Social media, 05 Colours
  Website icons, 1080 x 1080 profile pictures, and colour swatches (.ase for Adobe apps, .css, and a text sheet).

06 Motion
  Animated versions of the elements, as SVG files. They play in web browsers wherever an image can go; email
  clients do not play them. Each one rests on the original artwork and stays still for viewers who have asked
  their device for reduced motion.
  House of Evulv: the lotus floats and leans in a breeze over rippling water; the tree of life's DNA turns and
  climbs into the figure; the dove glides on a slow wingbeat while the olive branch moves in the wind.
  All seven colourways of each.
  Sentinels of Peace: the badge, its frame and the lettering draw themselves in once; the knights catch a passing
  light on their gold; the knights pair rises in four measured steps through a gold floor line.
  The generators that build these files are in the website repository, scripts/brand-motion.

New Sentinels of Peace files (October 2026)
  PNG: the two knights re-cut cleanly from the seal, with their legs, boots and plinths
  (knight-left_clean, knight-right_clean, knights-pair_clean).
  SVG: the seal's circle as a flat one-colour badge (badge, in white and Sentinel Navy), the circle alone to hold
  a portrait or a year (badge-frame), and the arched name with its star as a mark of its own (lettering, in gold,
  white and Sentinel Navy). Lettering set in Libre Bodoni Bold.

Typefaces: Libre Bodoni (headings) and Manrope (body), both free from Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License).
The existing logo files keep the lettering they were drawn with.
