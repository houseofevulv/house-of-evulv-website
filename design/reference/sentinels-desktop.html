Sentinels of Peace

Grand Honours for the individuals and organisations whose work prevents conflict, protects lives and helps communities in Nigeria recover.

Ceremony
Friday 18 December
Venue
Sheraton Lagos, Ikeja
Nominations close
Friday 20 November

Much of the work that keeps a community stable is never recorded.

Mediators, first responders, community leaders, public servants and civic groups prevent violence and help people recover from it. Few of them are ever named.

The Grand Honours names them, documents what they did, and puts them in the same room as the institutions able to support their work.

Recognise

Public honours for documented service in peace, security and community resilience.

Convene

Government, business, diplomatic, traditional, humanitarian and civic leaders in one room.

Amplify

Each honouree's story is written up and published so the work reaches more people.

Advance

Honourees are introduced to partners who can fund, extend or replicate what they started.

How an honouree is chosen

Criteria and panel members are published on 19 October, the day nominations open.

19 Oct to 20 Nov
Nominations
Anyone submits a nomination through this page.
23 to 27 Nov
Verification
We confirm each shortlisted claim with the nominator and one referee.
30 Nov to 4 Dec
Panel review
Five independent panellists from civil society, security, academia and the media score the shortlist.
Monday 7 Dec
Announcement
Honourees are named and their profiles published here.
Friday 18 Dec
Grand Honours
Presentation at the Sheraton Lagos Hotel, Ikeja.
