We honour and support the people who keep communities at peace.

House of Evulv is a social impact platform in Lagos. We run programmes in peace, healing and human development.

See the Sentinels of Peace

Our programmes reach elders and mediators, women rebuilding after loss and young people preparing to lead. The first is a national honours programme, held in Lagos this December.

Three commitments decide what we take on.

Every programme has to serve at least one of them, and we report against it.

Peace

Conditions where people and communities live with dignity and understand one another.

Healing

Helping people recover identity, hope and capacity after hard experiences.

Evolution

Helping people and institutions grow in how they think, lead and contribute.

The Sentinels of Peace Grand Honours
